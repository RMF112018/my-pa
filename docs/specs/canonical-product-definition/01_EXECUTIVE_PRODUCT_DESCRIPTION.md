# my-pa — Canonical Product Description

## Conclusion

my-pa is an **evidence-grounded executive continuity system**. It helps one executive know what matters, understand why, preserve context across projects and relationships, create missing evidence with minimal friction, and close loops without allowing model-generated inferences to silently become facts or actions.

It is not primarily a chatbot, CRM, note application, dashboard, knowledge graph, search box, or generic personal assistant. It integrates selected functions of those categories inside a stricter operating model built around evidence, attention, continuity, explicit authority, and durable receipts.

## Problem and user

Executive work is fragmented across email, calendar, contacts, project systems, financial records, schedules, documents, handwritten notes, conversations, memory, and informal commitments. Important matters compete with volume; context is repeatedly reconstructed; informal evidence is lost; polished summaries hide gaps; and tasks become disconnected from the decisions, people, projects, and evidence that made them meaningful.

The initial user is a single construction project executive operating across multiple projects, organizations, relationships, commitments, decisions, meetings, sources, and devices. Multi-user delegation and enterprise permissions are later product/security models.

## Product promise

> Know what matters, understand why, preserve continuity, create missing evidence with minimal friction, and close the loop—without allowing AI inferences to silently become facts or actions.

## Governing thesis and principles

> my-pa is an evidence-grounded executive continuity system that helps the user know what matters, understand why, preserve context across time, create new evidence with minimal friction, maintain continuity across projects and relationships, and close loops without allowing AI-generated inferences to silently become facts or actions.

1. Evidence before fluency.
2. Attention before volume.
3. Continuity before isolated transactions.
4. When the user is the source, preserve evidence first and structure it afterward.
5. AI may propose; it may not silently promote consequential facts or actions.
6. Projects, people, meetings, decisions, commitments, conversations, notes, and source records are different lenses over shared evidence.
7. The product remains useful without requiring chat.

## Canonical operating model

The primary product grammar remains:

**Today → Pulse → Situation → Frame → Trace or Review → Close**

- **Today** is the executive starting point.
- **Pulse** identifies and explains the few matters that deserve attention.
- **Situation** establishes operational context around a project, person, meeting, decision, issue, or mission.
- **Frame** assembles current evidence, commitments, decisions, questions, risks, alternatives, and unknowns.
- **Trace** reconstructs how current state developed.
- **Review** presents consequential proposed transitions for explicit authority.
- **Close** records outcome, receipt, remaining obligation, and next attention point.

Quick Capture does not replace this loop. Capture is a pervasive evidence-creation utility available from every stage, parallel to Reveal as the pervasive evidence-retrieval utility.

A secondary evidence lifecycle supports the product:

**Observe or Capture → Preserve → Interpret → Propose → Review or Promote → Surface → Trace → Close**

## Navigation and global utilities

Primary destinations remain:

- Today
- Situations
- Review
- Library
- System

Persistent global utilities:

- **Reveal** retrieves sources, records, relationships, timelines, and prior decisions with visible coverage and provenance.
- **Capture** creates user-authored evidence through **Quick Note** or **Conversation Log**.

Relationships, Projects, Captures, Conversations, Commitments, Decisions, Tasks, Knowledge, and Sources are Library collections, saved views, contextual workspaces, and Situation lenses—not separate top-level destinations.

## Quick Capture

Quick Capture is the path by which memory, observations, calls, informal meetings, relationship concerns, commitments, ideas, and project context become evidence.

The minimum interaction contract is fixed:

1. launch Quick Note or Conversation Log;
2. enter one unrestricted text field;
3. save;
4. exit after durable persistence;
5. continue backend processing asynchronously.

No title, project, participant, date, channel, tag, or structured metadata is required. Deterministic launch context may be attached and removed. Inferred context appears only after save as a proposal.

A saved Capture is authoritative evidence of exactly what the user committed. It is not proof that every described event or fact occurred. Editing creates a new immutable CaptureVersion.

Quick Note and Conversation Log are capture modes, not isolated products. An explicit Conversation Log may create a skeletal Conversation with unknown participants, channel, project, or occurred time. A Quick Note that appears conversational remains a proposal until reviewed or promoted under policy.

## Relationship Intelligence

Relationship Intelligence is the people-centered continuity domain inside my-pa. It is not a separate PRIE engine, database, frontend, or product. `PRIE` is historical terminology retained only for provenance.

It connects people, organizations, identity observations, unresolved identities, interactions, conversations, meetings, introductions, reciprocal commitments, private observations, concerns, preferences, follow-ups, projects, decisions, tasks, risks, issues, questions, timelines, and briefings.

It is not a CRM. It does not organize people into a sales pipeline, assign account ownership, infer loyalty, compute hidden sentiment or a composite relationship score, or continuously surveil communications. Transparent indicators may show observable conditions such as stale interaction, overdue reciprocal commitment, unresolved identity, or contradictory assertion.

Private relationship notes remain visibly user-authored observations. They do not silently become external facts. Public enrichment is disabled by default and requires separate policy and authorization.

## GoodNotes

GoodNotes is a source-first handwriting-to-knowledge capability. Original PDF pages and retained `.goodnotes` artifacts remain authoritative source evidence; OCR, transcription, classification, entities, tasks, commitments, decisions, and relationship events are derived or proposed.

The product processes new or materially changed page versions, preserves region-level provenance, records model/pipeline attempts, supports review and correction, and avoids mutating the source library. GoodNotes enters the same knowledge, review, project, relationship, and timeline domains.

The integrated product MVP includes source-neutral provenance/review contracts and one bounded synthetic GoodNotes page-to-region-to-accepted-assertion proof after the current read-only MCV substrate exists. Broad historical ingestion, advanced handwriting adaptation, challenger-model promotion, and live NAS use remain later gated capabilities.

## Evidence and authority

my-pa distinguishes authoritative source bytes or user-authored source text; product-owned canonical identities and accepted records; observed metadata; derived views; inferred proposals; reviewed promotions; contradictions; stale assertions; superseded versions; unavailable evidence; receipts; and audit events.

Confidence never substitutes for authority. High-confidence commitments, decisions, financial interpretations, dates, identity merges, and sensitive relationship conclusions remain review-gated because consequence, ambiguity, sensitivity, and reversibility matter.

## AI

AI is embedded in bounded extraction, classification, identity candidates, related-record candidates, summaries, contradiction candidates, briefings, follow-up proposals, attention recommendations, and selected-evidence synthesis.

AI receives no inherent tool authority. Captured, pasted, retrieved, or OCR-derived content is evidence data, not instruction authority. Model use is routed through purpose, source identity, selected fields/regions, classification, destination, provider/model/version, retention/training terms, policy decision, and audit receipt.

AI may not silently merge identities; create accepted commitments or decisions; interpret amounts/dates as canonical project truth; resolve contradictions; send messages; create calendar events; modify contacts or source systems; record calls; disclose private content to cloud models; use content for training; or delete/rewrite source evidence.

## Privacy and device strategy

Default posture for captures and sensitive observations is private/local, cloud-ineligible, training-ineligible, no lock-screen content preview, and no source text in analytics, logs, URLs, or crash breadcrumbs.

OS dictation into the text field is user-reviewed text input. Stored audio, meeting recording, live transcription, speaker diarization, automatic call interception, hidden recording, and consent inference are outside the MVP.

The first surface is a responsive web application with installable PWA behavior across iPhone, iPad, macOS, and Windows. MVP includes always-reachable Capture; mode routes; command palette; focused-app keyboard access; mobile full-height capture; tablet sheet; desktop modal/app window; supported PWA shortcuts; bounded encrypted append-only offline capture; foreground/resume synchronization; and WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility.

System-wide shortcuts, always-on-top windows, menu-bar/tray utilities, App Intents, widgets, native Share extensions, and stronger native key storage are later capabilities.

## Integrated MVP

The product MVP is complete only when the user can:

- begin in Today and understand what matters and why;
- enter Project or Relationship Situations without losing context;
- retrieve evidence through Reveal with visible coverage and authority;
- create Quick Notes and Conversation Logs from desktop and mobile;
- save without required metadata and exit after durable acknowledgment;
- recover an offline capture without duplication;
- search original capture text;
- inspect processing, proposal, review, and failure states;
- resolve or retain unresolved identities;
- review extracted commitments and decisions with exact evidence spans;
- view reciprocal commitments and interaction history in a Relationship workspace;
- inspect basic Project and Relationship timelines;
- review a bounded synthetic GoodNotes assertion from exact source region;
- understand System health and degraded sources;
- use the product without chat;
- pass trust, accessibility, privacy, security, recovery, and performance criteria.

The product MVP is broader than the repository’s active MCV. This publication does not reprioritize or authorize implementation.

## Long-term vision

my-pa becomes the user’s durable continuity layer across projects, people, decisions, obligations, sources, and time. Later capabilities may include deeper cross-source timelines, personalized handwriting recognition, native capture surfaces, user-initiated audio memos, public research under explicit policy, delegate review, and separately authorized external actions.

The invariant remains: **evidence is preserved, uncertainty is visible, and authority is explicit.**

## Rejected patterns

- chat-first product;
- card-wall dashboard as primary experience;
- mandatory capture form;
- separate PRIE application/store;
- CRM scoring or hidden sentiment;
- silent AI promotion;
- source mutation by default;
- broad offline canonical mutation;
- automatic call recording/interception;
- default cloud disclosure/training;
- native-first expansion;
- one navigation destination per object;
- premature microservices/graph/vector infrastructure;
- external actions derived directly from unreviewed evidence.

## Governed frontier-model access

`my-pa` can expose its capability plane to approved frontier-model clients without turning those clients into the product, the system of record, or a source of authority. ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, and future compatible clients can become additional reasoning and presentation surfaces for Reveal, evidence retrieval, bounded synthesis, and product-managed document work. They do not replace Today, Pulse, Situation, Frame, Trace, Review, Close, Library, Capture, or the first-party System controls.

The product value is not “connect every AI.” The value is continuity with choice: the user may select the frontier model best suited to a task while `my-pa` retains identity, authorization, evidence quality, source authority, uncertainty, lifecycle, audit, and recovery. A model can request a capability; the application decides whether the authenticated actor and client may use it, over which scope, for which purpose, and with which side-effect class.

Remote access preserves local control. NAS-backed source systems remain read-only and disclose exact scope, version, freshness, coverage, provenance, and limitations. A frontier client may create or revise only product-owned managed documents in a separately designated managed store. Those writes are versioned, idempotent, conflict-protected, auditable, reversible by default, and receipt-bearing. The connector never converts a source root into a write target.

Google Drive-like capability is pursued at the semantic level: discover, search, fetch, list, export representations, create managed folders/files, revise with history, relocate, copy, archive, restore, and collaborate through product-owned comments. `my-pa` does not imitate Google-specific storage, sharing, permission administration, Docs, Sheets, or Slides without real product-owned models and explicit authorization.

The connector advances the long-term product vision by letting the evidence operating model travel to the user's preferred reasoning surfaces. It does not change the category: `my-pa` remains an evidence-grounded executive continuity system.
