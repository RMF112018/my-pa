# Product Promise and Positioning

## Category recommendation

**Evidence-grounded executive continuity system**

Public use remains an operator decision.

## Promise

my-pa helps an executive know what matters, understand why, preserve context across projects and relationships, capture what would otherwise be lost, and close loops with evidence and explicit authority.

## Differentiation

- **Personal assistant:** answers/executes; my-pa preserves evidence, uncertainty, continuity, and authority.
- **Note app:** stores authoring; my-pa preserves source and creates reviewable linked domain proposals.
- **CRM:** manages pipeline/accounts; my-pa maintains relationship continuity without sales stages, ownership, surveillance, or scores.
- **Dashboard:** summarizes metrics; my-pa explains priority and supports evidence-to-review-to-closure.
- **Search:** retrieves matches; Reveal adds coverage/authority and Situation/Trace assemble context.
- **Knowledge graph:** models connections; my-pa centers governed evidence, attention, review, and closure.
- **Chatbot:** chat is optional; Today, Situations, Review, Library, System, Reveal, and Capture are primary.

## Value propositions

Executive clarity; continuity; memory capture; relationship reliability; decision integrity; source-backed trust; operational resilience.

## Rejected claims

Not “AI that runs your life,” autonomous agent, universal CRM, note replacement, automatic call recorder, surveillance product, replacement source of truth for external systems, or production-ready enterprise platform before single-user validation.

## Frontier-client extension

The connector strengthens the promise by allowing the user to bring preferred frontier reasoning surfaces to the same governed evidence plane. The positioning remains continuity and decision quality—not universal AI connectivity.

Supporting promise:

> Use the frontier model best suited to the task without surrendering evidence quality, source boundaries, privacy, or control.

This is differentiated from generic MCP servers and cloud drives because `my-pa` retains the context model, source authority, review/promotion rules, managed-document lifecycle, audit, and receipts. It is differentiated from a Google Drive clone because the product exposes evidence-aware, provider-neutral capabilities rather than reproducing Google-specific storage and permissions.

Rejected positioning claims include “your NAS becomes writable from any AI,” “works with every MCP client,” “complete Google Drive replacement,” “private because it uses Cloudflare,” and “model-generated documents are authoritative.” Each requires evidence or contradicts the product boundary.
