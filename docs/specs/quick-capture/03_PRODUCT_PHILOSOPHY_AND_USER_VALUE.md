## Principle

**When the user is the source, preserve the evidence first and structure it afterward.**

The product shorthand is **Capture first; structure later.**

## Why this matters

An evidence-grounded system has two ingestion problems:

1. evidence already exists in files, email, calendars, projects, financial systems, schedules, and other sources; and
2. evidence exists only in human memory after an observation or interaction.

The second category expires rapidly. The longer capture is delayed, the more detail, commitments, uncertainty, tone, and contextual meaning are lost. Requiring structured entry at that moment optimizes the database at the expense of evidence quality.

Quick Capture treats the first saved text as the durable evidence event. Structure is a downstream service, not an admission requirement.

## User-value statement

> Quick Capture lets the user preserve what happened in seconds, then lets my-pa organize, connect, and surface it without making the user complete a form.

## Product promises

1. **No filing tax before save.** One field is enough.
2. **No AI wait.** Persistence precedes processing.
3. **No silent interpretation.** Consequential inferences remain proposals.
4. **No lost original.** Source text and versions remain addressable.
5. **No context amnesia.** Launch context, time, device, and later links remain reconstructable.
6. **No forced review of everything.** Low-risk enrichment can remain noncanonical and useful without creating administrative work.
7. **No hidden disclosure.** Local content is not automatically cloud eligible, trainable, or notification safe.
8. **No accidental action.** A capture may propose work; it does not authorize execution.

## Relationship to the my-pa thesis

Quick Capture extends evidence-grounded executive continuity by adding an **authorship edge** to the evidence graph. It supports:

- Today and Pulse by supplying fresh observations and commitments;
- Situations and Frames by adding missing human context;
- Trace by preserving what the user knew and when;
- Review by creating explicit, source-bound proposals;
- Library and Reveal by making informal knowledge retrievable;
- Relationships by recording interactions, preferences, concerns, and reciprocal commitments;
- Projects by recording field observations, decisions, risks, schedule events, and financial facts.

## Product anti-patterns

Quick Capture must not become:

- a miniature CRM form;
- a mandatory tagging workflow;
- a chat conversation required before save;
- an automatic task generator;
- a hidden surveillance or relationship-scoring mechanism;
- a call-recording feature by implication;
- a dumping ground with no search, processing, or failure visibility;
- a model-generated rewrite that replaces the user’s words.

## Success measures

Product success is measured by:

- median launch-to-first-keystroke;
- median capture completion time;
- save success and recovery rate;
- percentage of captures requiring no pre-save metadata;
- percentage of extracted proposals traceable to exact spans;
- review precision and burden;
- successful later retrieval;
- accepted contextual links;
- false commitment/decision rate;
- duplicate and lost-capture rate;
- user-reported confidence that the original was preserved.

Raw capture volume is not a success metric by itself.
